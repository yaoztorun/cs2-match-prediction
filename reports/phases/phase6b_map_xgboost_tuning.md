# Phase 6B - Known-Map XGBoost Tuning (TRAIN-only chronological CV)

Generated by `scripts/models/map_xgboost_tuning_v1.py`. **40 candidates** (10 deterministic anchors + 30 `RandomState(42)` draws) x 4 expanding-window folds.

**Target: `team1_map_win`** - the winner of one specific, user-selected map. Not the pre-veto series task; these numbers are not comparable with the series models' accuracies.

## The two-stage fit per candidate x fold

```
INNER FIT             earliest ~85% of the fold's own train history (mirrored)
    v strictly earlier than
INNER EARLY STOP      latest ~15% of the fold's own train history (unmirrored)
    v strictly earlier than
OUTER FOLD VALIDATION scored ONLY after the refit - never consulted for tree count
```

1. Fit preprocessing on the augmented INNER FIT only; fit with `early_stopping_rounds=120` and `n_estimators=2500` (cap) against INNER EARLY STOP to learn `best_iteration`.
2. Record `best_iteration + 1` and **discard** that model.
3. Re-fit preprocessing on the FULL outer-fold TRAIN, mirror it, and refit a **new** model with `n_estimators = best_iteration + 1`, **no `eval_set`, no early stopping**.
4. Only then score the untouched outer-fold validation block.

Every map of a series - and every exact-timestamp group - stays entirely on one side of the inner boundary, asserted per fold. Block sizes:

| fold | inner-fit maps | inner-early-stop maps | outer-train maps (augmented) | validation maps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1038 | 183 | 1221 (2442) | 1544 |
| 2 | 2350 | 415 | 2765 (5530) | 1780 |
| 3 | 3863 | 682 | 4545 (9090) | 1609 |
| 4 | 5230 | 924 | 6154 (12308) | 1608 |

## Why the main validation partition cannot have influenced this

This script never opens `data/modeling/map_split_v1.csv`; it reads only the Phase 6A CV manifest, which contains TRAIN match_ids only. Early stopping consumes a block that lives entirely inside the fold's own training history, never the outer validation block and never the main validation partition.

## Selection rule (fixed before the search ran)

1) PRIMARY: lowest mean outer-fold log loss. 2) EQUIVALENCE: within 0.002 of the best mean log loss counts as tied. 3) highest mean ROC-AUC. 4) lowest mean Brier. 5) highest mean accuracy. 6) lower log-loss standard deviation. 7) COMPLEXITY (deterministic): lower max_depth -> higher min_child_weight -> higher gamma -> higher reg_lambda -> higher reg_alpha -> lower median effective tree count -> candidate_id. subsample/colsample_bytree are tunable predictive hyperparameters and are deliberately NOT used as a complexity ranking. Fit time never participates in selection.

`final_n_estimators = round(median(best_iteration + 1 across the 4 folds))`, also fixed in advance and derived from CV only.
